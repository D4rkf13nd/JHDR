About Us

Turning Your Ideas Into Beautifully Crafted Spaces

was founded in 2020, and its main objective is to offer the Best in the Industry quality and affordable products and solutions. Our expertise ranges from metal cutting and fabrication using the latest technology from Europe and Asian Countries. Starting with our significant products, consumables, supplies, and services, and a small office composed of 3 people, we have begun to meet the Philippine industry's needs in manufacturing and construction.

Today, JHDR operates two distinct divisions — Machine Tools and Prefab Container House. We became a subcontractor for prefab installation in 2022, and by 2023 had launched direct sales of prefab units nationwide.
